## Found & fixed (this commit)

**`lib/delta_calc/funding.ex` — float ordering in a "Pure Decimal only" module (3 sites).**
`funding_trend/1`, `compare_single_symbol/1`, and `rank_venues/1` ordered Decimal funding
rates by mapping each through `Decimal.to_float/1` (`Enum.max_by`/`min_by`/`sort_by`).
Converting money-domain Decimals to IEEE-754 floats purely to sort them violates the
project's core convention and can mis-order at the precision extremes.

Fixed to use `Decimal` as the comparison module — behavior-preserving:
- `Enum.max_by(rates, & &1, Decimal)` / `Enum.min_by(rates, & &1, Decimal)`
- `Enum.sort_by(fn {_v, rate} -> rate end, {:desc, Decimal})`

Verified: `mix compile --warnings-as-errors` clean; all 21 `funding_test.exs` tests pass.
