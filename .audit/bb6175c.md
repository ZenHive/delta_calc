## Finding — cross-cadence ranking bug (Codex, pri 8) → FILED as rmap task 35

`compare_single_symbol/2` selects `max_exchange`/`min_exchange`, `delta`, and the
`arbitrage_opportunity` flag from RAW per-period rates, but `annual_apr_delta/5` then annualizes
each venue at its OWN cadence and subtracts. With mixed cadences the annualized spread can go
NEGATIVE and the venue labels invert.

Verified trigger (read `lib/delta_calc/funding.ex:238-285` in-session):

    Funding.compare_funding_rates(
      %{hourly: Decimal.new("0.0001"), eight_hour: Decimal.new("0.0007")},
      %{hourly: 24, eight_hour: 3}
    )

ranks `:eight_hour` as max by raw rate (0.0007 > 0.0001), then
`annual_apr_delta = 0.0007*3*365*100 - 0.0001*24*365*100 = 76.65 - 87.60 = -10.95` — even though
the hourly venue has the HIGHER annualized rate (87.60% vs 76.65%). `max_exchange`/`min_exchange`
are mislabeled. Root design issue: `@arbitrage_threshold 0.0005` is a raw per-period value with no
defined meaning across cadences.

**Resolution:** Codex-only finding, verified real in-session. Per user decision during the audit,
filed as **rmap task 35** (not fixed in-audit) because the correct fix is a domain decision
(whether cross-cadence ranking/arbitrage operates on annualized rates, and the threshold's units).
The mixed-cadence `%{venue => periods}` form is brand-new (task 28) with no consumers yet, so the
fix is migration-cheap. Same-cadence behavior is unaffected (raw and annualized ranking agree when
cadence is constant).
