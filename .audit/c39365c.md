### 2. `mix precommit` never enforced warnings-as-errors — flag typo (serious)

names as the canonical gate — compiled warnings through without failing. `ci` and `check.dispatch`
had it right, which is why nothing surfaced. Pre-existing (scaffold commit `fb44929`, before this
range), but a hole in a gate the range relies on. **Fixed.**

decodes to a float and must be rejected.
